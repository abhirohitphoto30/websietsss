PEHLI BAAT

Wo muskaan

Sabse pehle jo baat najar aayi, wo tumhari muskaan thi. Ek aisi muskaan jo kisi bhi kamre ko halka kar de, jo baaton ko aasaan bana de. Sach kahun, wahi se shuruaat hui thi.

✦
DOOSRI BAAT

Wo aankhen

Tumhari aankhon mein ek sukoon hai, ek imandaari hai jo aajkal kam hi dikhti hai. Jab tum baat karte waqt seedha dekhti ho, lagta hai jaise sach mein koi sun raha hai, samajh raha hai.

❀
TEESRI BAAT

Tumhara tareeka

Khoobsurati sirf chehre mein nahi hoti, wo tumhare uthne-baithne mein, baat karne ke andaaz mein, chhoti chhoti cheezon ko dhyaan se sunne mein bhi hoti hai. Tum mein wo sab hai — bina kisi koshish ke.

✧
CHAUTHI BAAT

Sirf panch din

Hum ek doosre ko jyada nahi jaante, ye sach hai. Par kabhi kabhi dil ko waqt ka intezaar nahi hota. Jo thoda sa mile ho, wahi kaafi tha ye samajhne ke liye ki tum khaas ho.

AAKHri BAAT

Bas itna kehna tha

Sania, tum jitni khoobsurat dikhti ho, utni hi pyaari andar se bhi lagti ho. Ye chand shabd bas ek chhota sa tareeka the tumhe ye batane ka. Umeed hai tumhare chehre pe muskaan aayi hogi.

♡

— Rahul Sharma
